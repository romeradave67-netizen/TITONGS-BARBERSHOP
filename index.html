HISTORY

 Titong Barber Shop, established in January 2019, began as a small neighborhood barbershop dedicated to quality haircuts and friendly service. Over the years, it has grown into a trusted local spot, known for skilled grooming, warm customer care, and a welcoming community vibe.

LIFE OF THE OWNER

 Starting at just 23 years old, John Lyster Trapero bravely stepped into the world of barbering with nothing but passion, hard work, and a dream of building something of his own. Growing up, he was always fascinated by clean cuts and sharp styles, and he spent countless hours learning from local barbers and practicing his craft on friends. With determination, he saved enough to open a small barbershop, where every haircut became a chance to prove his skill and dedication. Despite challenges, John stayed committed, treating every customer like family and slowly building a loyal community around his shop. Over time, his barbershop became more than a business—it became a place where stories were shared, confidence was restored, and his talent truly shined. Today, John continues to grow his craft, inspiring others with his journey from a young dreamer to a respected barber in his community.

SUCCESS STORY OF THE BUSINESS

 John Lyster Trapero’s barbershop business grew from a small, single-chair setup into a well-known grooming spot in his community. Through dedication, skill, and genuine care for his customers, he slowly built a loyal clientele who trusted his hands for clean and stylish cuts. As the years passed, he upgraded his tools, expanded his shop, and even trained young barbers who looked up to him. His solid commitment to quality service and friendly customer relationships became the foundation of his success, turning his once-humble barbershop into a thriving and respected business.

CHALLENGES MET BY THE OWNER

 Despite his success, John faced many challenges on his journey. In the beginning, he struggled with limited resources, often working long hours to save enough for better equipment and to keep the shop running. Competition from bigger and more modern barbershops also tested his confidence, but he stayed focused on improving his craft. There were times when customer traffic slowed, and managing staff became difficult, yet he learned to adapt through patience, discipline, and continuous learning. These challenges strengthened his character and shaped him into a resilient and determined business owner.

THE BUSINESS 10 YEARS AFTER

Ten years from now, Titong’s Barbershop is envisioned as a well-established and respected grooming brand in the community, known not only for quality haircuts but also for excellent customer experience. The shop may expand to a larger space or open additional branches, offering more services such as premium grooming, styling, and barber training programs for young barbers. With continuous improvement, modern tools, and strong customer relationships, the business will likely become a go-to barbershop for different generations. By staying true to its values of skill, hard work, and community care, Titong’s Barbershop can grow into a long-term, sustainable business that continues to inspire and serve people for years to come.

HOW DID THE GOVERNMENT HELP THE BUSINESS GROW

The Philippine government has helped barbershops and other small businesses grow by providing support programs, training, and financial assistance. Agencies like the Department of Trade and Industry (DTI) make it easier for barbershop owners to register their business, access free seminars on entrepreneurship, marketing, and customer service, and join trade fairs to promote their services.
